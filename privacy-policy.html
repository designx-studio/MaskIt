Maskit Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Local processing only. Maskit helps reduce accidental exposure of sensitive data. It does not guarantee protection. All detection happens in your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your page content or clipboard data.

Supported sites

Maskit currently runs only on ChatGPT and Gmail. It does not inject into other websites unless permissions are expanded in a future release.

What Maskit accesses

  • Text you paste, copy, or type on supported pages while the extension is active
  • Your extension settings (toggles, site filters, custom regex rules)
  • Aggregate redaction counters stored locally
  • The hostname of the current tab to apply site allow/block lists

What Maskit does not do

  • Send page content, clipboard text, or detected values to external servers
  • Store raw matched text, clipboard logs, or redaction history
  • Track browsing history for advertising
  • Read the clipboard outside paste/copy events you trigger

Data storage

Settings sync through chrome.storage.sync via your Google account. Only configuration is synced — never matched content.

Statistics (total counts by type and source) are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device. You can reset stats anytime.

Clipboard behavior

Maskit only inspects clipboard text during paste or copy events inside supported editors. It does not run background clipboard monitoring.

Custom regex rules

Rules you create are stored in your settings and executed locally. Maskit rejects overly long or potentially unsafe regex patterns.

Permissions

  • storage — save settings and local counters
  • host permissions — run only on ChatGPT and Gmail

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the extension publisher through the Chrome Web Store listing support channel.
