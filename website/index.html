Architecture

Keep the boundary close to the data.

Maskit does not need a cloud proxy to protect a paste into ChatGPT or a local agent call. The enforcement point stays on the device, while future management layers can distribute policy and collect non-sensitive evidence.

Enterprise controls

Security teams get evidence without collecting the secret.

Define app and context policies, choose allow, redact, or block actions, retain structured audit events, and build toward shared governance without turning raw prompts into a central data lake.
