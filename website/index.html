Foundation
Security by architecture

Keep raw context local. Make policy visible.

Maskit is defense in depth, not a guarantee. The trust model is explicit: inspect locally, avoid raw prompt storage, publish the limits, and make the decision explainable.

What stays local

Raw prompts, clipboard text, file contents, and matched secret values. Audit events store metadata and salted hashes instead of the raw match.

What teams can inspect

Data type, confidence, severity, action, policy name, app context, timestamp, and reason. Central fleet management is a future layer, not a hidden claim.
