What gets caught.

Every category maps to real rules in the shared engine.

PII

Email, phone, SSN, passport, and IPv4 addresses.

Financial

Cards with Luhn validation, bank accounts and IBANs, plus M-Pesa references.

Secrets

30+ named provider formats across OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, GCP, Azure, GitHub, GitLab, Slack, Stripe, Twilio, SendGrid, Cloudflare, Auth0, Firebase, Docker Hub, and more.

Custom

Local regex rules with length and unsafe-pattern checks.

Beyond detection

Protection is more than a regex.

Policies, audit, control.

Per-app and per-context actions let teams choose allow, redact, or block. The local audit log stores hashed tokens, chain hashes, retention settings, and CSV export. An admin killswitch can restrict AI tools by schedule or duration.
