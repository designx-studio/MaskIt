01 / The risk

Every AI handoff is a decision your perimeter cannot see.

The data does not need to be stolen to become exposed. It only needs to be pasted into the wrong assistant, read by the wrong agent, or carried through the wrong tool call.

01
Prompts carry more than words.

Customer records, credentials, code, and internal context often arrive together.

02
Agents act without a human at every step.

Tool calls and file access create new decisions beyond the chat window.

03
Legacy controls miss the handoff.

A network proxy cannot govern a local paste or unmanaged desktop workflow it never sees.

02 / Protection model

From sensitive input to safe interaction.

Maskit makes the decision close to the source. The model is simple enough to explain, and consistent enough to enforce across products.

InputHuman or agent

Prompt, file, clipboard, resource, tool argument.

DecisionMaskit engine

Detect, validate, classify, and apply policy locally.

OutputSafe AI interaction

Allow, redact, block, or record the decision.
