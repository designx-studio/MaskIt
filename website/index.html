The commercial wedge

One report. Three answers.

MSPs need to explain what AI tools are present, what risks were observed, what controls acted, and what remains unknown. MaskIt turns local inventory, policy state, detection events, response actions, and risk calculation into one AI Security Report.

Export JSON, Markdown, or CSV locally. A future MSP console can collect the same privacy-preserving object without rebuilding the endpoint model.
