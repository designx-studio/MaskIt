Maskit Privacy Policy

Maskit processes sensitive content locally. The browser extension uses chrome.storage.local, not sync storage.

What we do not collect

Maskit does not upload prompts, clipboard text, raw matched values, or telemetry to a Maskit-hosted service.

Supported browser hosts

ChatGPT, chat.openai.com, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Cursor host patterns configured in the extension manifest.
